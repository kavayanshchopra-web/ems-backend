📋 Setup Checklist

Real-time step synchronization with your Samsung smartphone.

1

Open Phone Dialer

From Home Screen, tap the green Phone app icon.

2

Tap 3 Dots (⋮) → Settings

Select the 3-dots menu top right, then tap Settings ⚙️.

3

Tap "Record calls"

In Call Settings, tap on the Record calls row.

4

Enable "Auto record calls"

Turn switch to ON (Green). All calls auto-sync to OmniFlow!
