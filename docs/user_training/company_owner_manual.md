# 🏢 Company Owner User Training Manual (EMS System)

Welcome to the **Company Owner Control Manual**. As the business owner, you manage employee registers, configure fuel reimbursement rates, audit/approve all daily shift expense payouts, and access system-wide audit registries.

---

## 🔑 Key Responsibilities & Flows

### 1. **Assigning Vehicles & Dynamic Fuel Rates**
- **What it is:** Assign specific vehicles to employees (Bike, Car, SUV) so their travel allowances are calculated correctly.
- **How to perform:**
  - Go to **`GPS & Field Attendance`** from the left menu.
  - From the top dropdown, select the target employee card (e.g. *Amit Kumar*).
  - Scroll down to the **`🚗 ASSIGN SHIFT VEHICLE`** dropdown box.
  - Choose the vehicle:
    - **Bike:** Reimbursed at ₹6/KM (default)
    - **Car:** Reimbursed at ₹12/KM
    - **SUV:** Reimbursed at ₹18/KM
  - Once selected, the system dynamically recalculates the total shift travel allowance claims.

---

### 2. **Configuring Fuel Reimbursement Rates**
- **What it is:** Change the price per KM rate for each vehicle type according to company policy.
- **How to perform:**
  - Go to the **Manager Settings Panel** at the bottom of the left column.
  - Locate the **Fuel Rates Settings** block.
  - Input new rates (e.g. change Bike rate from ₹6 to ₹8 per KM).
  - Click **`Save Rates`**. All mileage calculations will instantly adjust to these new values.

---

### 3. **Auditing & Approving Daily Shift Expenses (Drag & Drop Supported)**
- **What it is:** Review expense submissions (tolls, breakfast, lunch, dinner, miscellaneous) logged by employees and approve/reject them for payout.
- **How to perform:**
  - Open the selected employee card or audit timeline feed.
  - Find the **`💰 DAILY SHIFT EXPENSES`** box.
  - You will see the claim breakdown (e.g., Toll total, meals, total amount claim) along with a link to view the uploaded toll slip image proof.
  - **Uploading Slips (Drag & Drop):** If adding extra receipt proofs, you can drag receipt images directly into the dashed upload area instead of browsing.
  - Select the approval status from the dropdown:
    - `Change Approval Status` -> Choose **`Approve Payout`** (turns Green) or **`Reject Claim`** (turns Red).
  - Approved status instantly locks the reimbursement claim for payroll processing.

---

## 🌐 New Global Utilities & Shortcuts

### 4. **Language Selector (English / Hindi / Hinglish)**
- **How to perform:**
  - In the top header panel, locate the **`🌐 LANG`** dropdown box.
  - Swap languages dynamically (e.g., choose **हिंदी** or **Hinglish**). All sidebar links and menus will instantly translate to keep navigation intuitive for local staff.

### 5. **Global Search Console (Ctrl + K)**
- **How to perform:**
  - Press **`Ctrl + K`** (or `Cmd + K` on Mac) or click the search box in the top-header.
  - An search overlay modal will display. Type the query (e.g., "Amit", "Noida", "Audit").
  - Click on the search item to go directly to the target tab.
  - Press **`Esc`** at any time to instantly close this overlay or any other modal dialog boxes.

### 6. **Accessing the System Audit Registry**
- **How to perform:**
  - Click on **`📜 System Audit Logs`** in the dashboards list.
  - You can monitor security logs showing who performed which actions (e.g. login updates, rate adjustments).
  - Click **`🧹 Clear Log Registry`** to flush session event logs.
