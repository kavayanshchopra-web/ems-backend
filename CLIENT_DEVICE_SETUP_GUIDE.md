# 📱 OmniFlow Companion: Universal Phone Setup & Call Recording Guide
> **Official Step-by-Step Guide for Clients & Telecalling Agents**  
> *Follow your phone brand below. Setup takes less than 60 seconds.*

---

## 🚀 Quick Checklist (Before You Start)
1. Install **OmniFlow Live Companion App** on your phone.
2. Grant all requested permissions:
   - ✅ **Phone & Call Log** (to track calls and calculate duration)
   - ✅ **All Files Storage Access** (allows the app to auto-detect recordings without asking you to pick folders!)
   - ✅ **Battery Optimization: Unrestricted** (keeps syncing alive in the background)
3. Turn **ON** Auto-Call Recording in your phone dialer using your brand guide below.

---

## 1. 📱 Samsung Galaxy Phones (Galaxy S, A, M, F, Z Series)
Samsung has the **best built-in call recording system**. It records both sides in studio quality without any voice announcements.

### How to Turn ON Auto Call Recording:
1. Open the default green **Samsung Phone (Phone by Samsung)** app.
2. Tap the **3 vertical dots (⋮)** in the top-right corner next to the search bar.
3. Tap **Settings**.
4. Tap **Record calls**.
5. Tap **Auto record calls** (tap the text, not just the switch).
6. Turn the switch **ON** and select **"All calls"**.
7. **That's it!** You're 100% ready. OmniFlow will automatically pick up and sync all recordings.

---

## 2. 📱 Vivo & iQOO Phones (V, Y, T, X Series & iQOO Z/Neo)
Vivo and iQOO phones have a native high-definition recording engine built right into the device.

### Step A: If your phone already uses the Vivo Phone app:
1. Open the **Phone** app > Tap **Settings** (or 3 dots ⋮).
2. Tap **Record settings**.
3. Select **"Record all calls automatically"**.

### Step B: If your phone has Google Dialer (Blue phone icon):
Google dialer blocks silent recording. Vivo has provided an official 1-tap code to restore Vivo's official dialer:
1. Open your phone dialer pad.
2. Type this exact code: `*#*#5566888#*#*`
3. A screen will appear: **"Enable Alternate Phone and Contacts"**.
4. Turn the switch **ON** > Tap **OK** > Tap **Set as default**.
5. Now open this newly enabled Phone app > Go to **Settings > Record settings > Record all calls automatically**.
6. **Done!** OmniFlow will now auto-sync every single call!

---

## 3. 📱 OnePlus, OPPO & Realme Phones (ColorOS, OxygenOS, Realme UI)
Newer models of OnePlus, Oppo, and Realme come with Google Dialer out of the box. The manufacturer has released their own official dialer on Google Play Store called **ODialer**.

### How to Enable 100% HD Silent Call Recording:
1. Open Google Play Store and search for **"ODialer by ColorOS"** (or use this link: [Download ODialer on Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.oplus.dialer)).
2. Tap **Install**.
3. Open **ODialer** and tap **"Set as Default Phone app"**.
4. In ODialer, tap the **3 dots (⋮)** in the top-right corner > Tap **Settings**.
5. Tap **Call recording** > Turn **ON** **"Record all calls"**.
6. **Done!** OmniFlow Companion will automatically scan and upload your calls without any announcements!

---

## 4. 📱 Xiaomi, Redmi & POCO Phones (MIUI / HyperOS)
Xiaomi devices support automatic call recording through their native dialer or MIUI Sound Recorder.

### If using Xiaomi Native Dialer:
1. Open the **Phone** app.
2. Tap the **Settings gear (⚙️)** or 3 lines in the corner.
3. Tap **Call recording**.
4. Turn **ON** both:
   - **Call recording notification** (optional)
   - **Record calls automatically**
5. Under "Selected numbers", make sure **"All numbers"** is selected.

### If using Google Phone app on Redmi/POCO:
1. Open **Phone** app > Tap 3 dots (⋮) > **Settings**.
2. Tap **Call recording**.
3. Under "Always record", tap **"Numbers not in your contacts"** -> Turn **ON**.
4. Tap **"Selected numbers"** -> Turn **ON** -> Tap **Choose a contact** to add your primary business numbers.

---

## 5. 📱 Tecno, Infinix & itel (HiOS / XOS / itelOS)
Transsion phones have automatic call recording built directly into their phone settings without restrictions.

### How to Enable:
1. Open the default **Phone** app.
2. Tap the **Settings icon (⚙️)** in the top-right corner.
3. Look for **"Auto call recording"** (or "Automatic recording").
4. Turn the switch **ON**.
5. All calls will now be automatically saved and synced into OmniFlow CRM!

---

## 6. 📱 Motorola & Google Pixel Phones
Motorola and Google Pixel strictly use the Google Phone app.

### Instructions:
1. Open **Phone by Google** app > Tap 3 dots (⋮) > **Settings** > **Call recording**.
2. Enable **"Numbers not in your contacts"** to record unknown clients automatically.
3. **Recommendation for Office Fleets:** For commercial telecalling teams making hundreds of customer calls daily, **Samsung, Vivo, or OnePlus/Oppo** devices are recommended for 100% two-sided recording without Google restrictions.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### Q1: Do I need to select a storage folder in the OmniFlow app?
**NO!** OmniFlow Companion uses **Zero-Touch Auto-Discovery**. As long as you have granted "All Files Access", our app automatically discovers where your phone saves recordings and syncs them in 0.5 seconds.

#### Q2: The CRM shows the call log and duration, but says "⚠️ No Recording". Why?
This means call recording is currently **turned OFF** in your phone's dialer settings. Follow your brand's guide above to turn ON "Auto record calls", and your next call will record and upload instantly!

#### Q3: Does OmniFlow work with Dual SIM phones (SIM 1 and SIM 2)?
**YES, 100%!** OmniFlow Companion has full multi-layer SIM detection. When you call from SIM 2, the CRM will accurately display `SIM (SIM 2)`.
